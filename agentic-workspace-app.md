# DraftLane product direction

DraftLane connects the first sketch to a plan a team can act on.

## Visual identity

- A geometric D with two forward lanes represents momentum from idea to execution.
- Mint accents, ink backgrounds, and a drafting grid establish a calm working environment.
- Canvas, documents, and the team board share the same workspace context.

## First release

- Describe a project in plain language or by voice.
- Draft diagrams and documents with the workspace agent.
- Refine the plan together with live collaborative editing.
- Organize workspaces by status and share read-only views.
